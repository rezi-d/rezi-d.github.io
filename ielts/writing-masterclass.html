⚖️ 4 Kriteria Penentu Nilai IELTS Writing (Bobot 25% Tiap Kriteria)

Official Rubric Standard

Setiap tulisan Anda dievaluasi secara independen pada 4 skala penilaian berikut. Kelemahan pada satu kriteria akan langsung menurunkan skor akumulasi:

1. TA / TR 25%
Task Achievement (T1) / Task Response (T2)

• Task 1: Menyeleksi tren makro utama (*clear overview*), membandingkan data pokok tanpa terjebak mencatat seluruh angka mentah.
• Task 2: Menjawab seluruh bagian pertanyaan prompt, menyajikan posisi tesis yang jelas dari awal sampai akhir tanpa argumen mengambang.

2. CC 25%
Coherence & Cohesion

Kelancaran alur logika antarparagraf dan antarkalimat. Tiap paragraf wajib memiliki satu fokus topik (*central topic sentence*). Penggunaan kata hubung (*cohesive devices*) mengalir wajar tanpa terkesan dipaksakan (*mechanical linking*).

3. LR 25%
Lexical Resource

Variasi perbendaharaan kata akademik (*less common lexical items*), penguasaan kolokasi alami (*collocation awareness*), keakuratan ejaan kata (*spelling*), dan keberanian memparafrase kalimat prompt tanpa menyalin mentah-mentah.

4. GRA 25%
Grammatical Range & Accuracy

Kombinasi struktur kalimat kompleks (klausa relatif, pasif ilmiah, kalimat inversi, klausa partisip, dan kalimat pengandaian). Mayoritas kalimat wajib bebas dari kesalahan gramatikal mayor (*error-free sentences*).

📊 Task 1 Academic: Formula 3-Langkah & 6 Tipe Visual Data

Writing Reference Hal. 102–106
1

Paragraf 1: Parafrase Prompt

Tulis ulang kalimat soal menggunakan sinonim C1 dan ubah struktur sintaksis (1-2 kalimat saja). Jangan menyalin kata per kata dari instruksi lembar soal!

"The line graph illustrates fluctuations in electricity consumption across three separate geographical regions between 1995 and 2015."
2

Paragraf 2: "The Golden Overview"

KUNCI MUTLAK BAND 7.0+: Tuliskan 2 tren makro paling mencolok, tahapan awal/akhir proses, atau perubahan tata ruang utama. Dilarang keras menyertakan angka detail pada paragraf ini!

"Overall, solar power experienced the most dramatic surge, whereas coal-dependent generation suffered a steady decline over the period."
3

Paragraf 3 & 4: Detail & Komparasi Data

Kelompokkan data secara logis. Cantumkan angka presisi, gunakan perbandingan komparatif (*in stark contrast, twofold increase, plateaued at*), atau tahapan berurutan.

"Specifically, wind power output grew twofold from 15 GWh in 2005 to precisely 30 GWh in 2015, after which it plateaued."

Karakteristik 6 Format Visual Data IELTS Task 1 (Cambridge p. 102–106)

1. Line Graph (Tren Waktu):

Fokus pada pergerakan garis dari waktu ke waktu: surged, plummeted, fluctuated wildly, reached a plateau, peaked at.

2. Bar Chart (Komparasi Kategori):

Membandingkan nilai antar kelompok/negara: outperformed, accounted for double the share, lagged significantly behind.

3. Pie Chart (Pangsa & Proporsi):

Menjelaskan pembagian persentase dari 100%: represented the vast majority of, comprised a mere fraction, constituted roughly one-third.

4. Data Table (Matriks Angka Padat):

Pilah data tertinggi, terendah, dan anomali utama. Jangan laporkan semua sel angka! Fokus pada perbandingan ekstrem.

5. Process Diagram (Alur Siklus / Pabrik):

Gunakan Passive Voice dan urutan sekuensial: Initially, raw materials are sorted; subsequently, the mixture undergoes heating...

6. Comparative Maps (Peta Perubahan):

Kosakata tata ruang & arah mata angin: was demolished, repurposed, expanded northward, gave way to a pedestrianized zone.

Kamus Dinamika Perubahan Data C1 (Verba, Nomina & Adjektiva)

Dinamika Kata Kerja (*Verbs*) Kata Benda (*Nouns*) Adjektiva / Adverbia C1 Contoh Preposisi Wajib
📈 Melonjak Cepat rocket, surge, soar, leap a sharp surge, an exponential leap exponentially, substantially surged by 25% to 80 GWh
📉 Menukik Tajam plummet, plunge, slump, tumble a precipitous drop, a steep decline precipitously, drastically plummeted from 50 to 12
➡️ Stabil / Fluktuasi level off, plateau, fluctuate wildly a period of stability, fluctuations steadily, marginally, consistently plateaued at approximately 40%

3 Model Jawaban Band 8.5 Teranotasi Task 1 (Line Graph · Process · Maps)

Model 1 · Line Graph: Renewable vs Fossil Power (2000–2020) 168 Kata · Band 8.5

[Introduction]: The line graph illustrates the proportion of electricity generated from renewable and fossil fuel sources across three distinct European nations over a twenty-year period between 2000 and 2020.

[The Golden Overview]: Overall, it is immediately evident that renewable energy production experienced a substantial upward trajectory across all surveyed countries, whereas dependence on fossil fuel generation contracted markedly over the timeframe. Notably, Sweden established an overwhelming lead in green energy output by the end of the period.

[Body 1 - Clean Energy]: Commencing in 2000, Sweden generated precisely 30% of its power from sustainable installations. This figure rose steadily to plateau at 45% in 2010, before surging exponentially to culminate at a peak of nearly 70% in 2020. In stark contrast, Germany's renewable share started at a modest 10%, yet followed a comparable positive gradient, eventually reaching 35%.

[Body 2 - Fossil Comparison]: Conversely, non-renewable generation exhibited an inverse pattern. Coal-dependent output in the United Kingdom plummeted precipitously from an initial 65% in 2000 to a mere 15% two decades later, representing the most dramatic decline among the examined nations.

Model 2 · Process Flowchart: Plastic Bottle Recycling to Fleece Fabric 174 Kata · Band 8.5

[Introduction]: The diagram illustrates the cyclical sequence of stages involved in transforming discarded post-consumer plastic beverage bottles into synthetic polyester fleece apparel.

[The Golden Overview]: Overall, the production cycle encompasses a multi-step industrial operation comprising six principal phases, commencing with waste collection and classification, progressing through mechanical pulverization and thermal processing, and culminating in yarn spinning and fabric manufacture.

[Body 1 - Initial Preparation & Flaking]: In the initial phase, discarded polyethylene bottles are deposited into recycling containers, collected by sanitation trucks, and conveyed to an automated sorting facility. After non-recyclable contaminants are eliminated, the plastic bottles undergo high-pressure cleaning to remove residual liquids and labels. Once sanitized, they are directed into an industrial grinder where they are shredded into fine polymer flakes.

[Body 2 - Thermal Extrusion & Weaving]: Following mechanical shredding, the flakes are placed in a smelting furnace and heated until molten. The liquid polymer is then forced through a micro-perforated spinneret to form continuous filament fibers. Having been cooled and drawn to enhance tensile strength, these synthetic strands are wound onto spools. In the final phase, the yarn is woven on specialized industrial looms into durable fleece fabric, which is subsequently tailored into winter clothing items.

Model 3 · Comparative Maps: Town of Shipley Redevelopment (1995 vs 2025 Plan) 178 Kata · Band 8.5

[Introduction]: The accompanying maps depict the coastal settlement of Shipley as it existed in 1995 alongside projected municipal modifications scheduled for implementation by 2025.

[The Golden Overview]: Overall, the urban layout is slated to undergo extensive modernization, shifting from a primarily industrial and derelict port into an eco-friendly residential and leisure hub, characterized by pedestrianization and expanded recreational amenities.

[Body 1 - Northern & Commercial Zones]: The most striking alteration is evident in the northern district, where a disused textile factory and adjoining railway sidings from 1995 will be demolished. In their place, planners intend to erect a sprawling multi-story commercial complex bordered by high-density residential apartments. The central thoroughfare, previously dominated by heavy vehicle congestion, has been repurposed into an expansive pedestrian promenade lined with trees.

[Body 2 - Waterfront & Infrastructure]: Moving to the southern coastline, the historic fishing harbor has been converted into a modern leisure marina accommodating private yachts. Furthermore, vacant scrubland situated to the east of the pier is designated for development into a public botanical park and coastal cycle path, thereby significantly upgrading green civic infrastructure.

Task 2: Alokasi 40 Menit · Minimal 250 Kata · Bobot 2/3 Nilai Total

🧱 Task 2 Essay: Arsitektur Paragraf PEEL & 3 Model Esai Band 8.5

Writing Reference Hal. 107–111

Dalam kriteria Coherence & Cohesion dan Task Response, setiap paragraf badan (*body paragraph*) harus berfokus pada **satu ide sentral yang dikembangkan tuntas** melalui struktur PEEL (Point, Explanation, Evidence, Link Back):

P

Point (Topic Sentence)

Kalimat pembuka yang merangkum satu ide pokok paragraf secara tegas.

"The primary justification for subsidising higher education lies in macroeconomic competitiveness."
E

Explanation

Jelaskan "mengapa" dan "bagaimana" ide tersebut bekerja secara logis mendalam.

"A highly educated workforce stimulates technological innovation, which in turn attracts foreign direct investment."
E

Evidence / Example

Beri studi kasus, data riil, atau ilustrasi konkret yang kredibel dan logis.

"For instance, South Korea's heavy state investment in engineering faculties underpinned its transition into a high-tech powerhouse."
L

Link Back

Kaitkan kembali penjelasan tersebut ke tesis utama esai Anda.

"Consequently, state subsidies for universities represent an indispensable investment rather than a fiscal burden."

Formula Struktur 4 Format Soal Esai Task 2 (Cambridge p. 107–111)

1. Opinion Essay (To what extent do you agree or disagree?):

Wajib mengambil posisi tegas di paragraf pendahuluan (*clear thesis statement*). Format terbaik: Intro (Tesis) → Body 1 (Argumen 1) → Body 2 (Argumen 2 / Konsesi terbatas) → Conclusion (Penegasan ulang).

2. Discussion Essay (Discuss both views and give your opinion):

Wajib membahas kedua pandangan secara adil sebelum menyatakan pandangan pribadi: Intro → Body 1 (Pandangan A secara objektif) → Body 2 (Pandangan B & mengapa Anda memihaknya) → Conclusion.

3. Advantages & Disadvantages (Do advantages outweigh disadvantages?):

Bukan sekadar mencatat daftar kelebihan/kekurangan, melainkan menimbang bobot dampaknya: Mengapa keuntungan jangka panjang lebih signifikan daripada kerugian jangka pendeknya.

4. Two-Part / Problem & Solution Essay:

Menjawab dua pertanyaan terpisah pada prompt soal: Intro → Body 1 (Analisis 2 akar penyebab utama) → Body 2 (2 Solusi kebijakan nyata yang saling berkorelasi) → Conclusion.

3 Model Esai Lengkap Band 8.5 Task 2 (Discussion · Opinion · Problem-Solution)

Model 1 · Discussion: Vocational Skills vs Broader Mandate in Universities 285 Kata · Band 8.5

[Paragraf 1 - Introduction & Thesis]: It is frequently debated whether higher education institutions should solely prioritize vocational proficiencies or serve a broader intellectual mandate. While preparing graduates for immediate employment is undeniably vital for national productivity, I firmly contend that universities must also foster critical inquiry and holistic democratic awareness.

[Paragraf 2 - Body 1 (PEEL - Vocational Side)]: On the one hand, advocates of market-driven curricula argue that the fundamental measure of tertiary education is its employment outcome. With tuition fees escalating globally, students legitimately expect tangible returns on their financial commitments. Aligning coursework with commercial demands ensures graduates possess applicable technical competencies, thereby curtailing corporate onboarding costs. For instance, computer science programs developed in partnership with technology firms guarantee immediate workplace productivity, which directly accelerates economic modernization.

[Paragraf 3 - Body 2 (PEEL - Broader Civic Purpose)]: On the other hand, reducing universities to mere job-training centers overlooks their foundational civic imperative. Academic institutions are traditional bastions of philosophical debate, scientific discovery, and ethical discourse. A comprehensive education encompassing humanities and theoretical inquiry equips individuals with cognitive flexibility and analytical empathy. In an era increasingly dominated by automated algorithms, these adaptable soft skills are arguably far more durable than rigid technical specialisations that risk rapid obsolescence.

[Paragraf 4 - Conclusion]: In conclusion, although calibrating academic degrees to labor market realities is essential, it should not supersede the broader developmental mission of tertiary institutions. A balanced university system must cultivate both lucrative occupational proficiencies and the intellectual acumen necessary to navigate an unpredictable future.

Model 2 · Opinion: Artificial Intelligence and Human Labor Disruption 281 Kata · Band 8.5

[Paragraf 1 - Introduction & Thesis]: A growing faction of commentators contends that rapid advancements in artificial intelligence and machine learning pose an unprecedented menace to global employment, arguing that the resultant social displacement will eclipse any economic benefits. I completely disagree with this pessimistic prognosis, as historical precedent and economic theory indicate that technological revolutions ultimately generate far more employment opportunities than they eliminate.

[Paragraf 2 - Body 1 (PEEL - Job Elimination Counter-Rebuttal)]: Granted, skeptics rightly point out that automation precipitates acute frictional unemployment in sectors characterized by repetitive cognitive or manual duties. Data-entry personnel, customer service representatives, and assembly workers inevitably face temporary dislocation as corporations adopt automated algorithms to maximize operational margins. However, interpreting this initial structural adjustment as permanent societal destruction fails to account for the dynamic adaptability of modern economies. Rather than rendering human labor obsolete, automation primarily shifts workforce allocation towards higher-order supervisory and analytical roles.

[Paragraf 3 - Body 2 (PEEL - New Industries Creation)]: More fundamentally, emerging technological paradigms invariably act as catalysts for entire industries that were previously unimaginable. Just as the advent of personal computing eradicated typing pools while creating millions of lucrative positions in software engineering and digital marketing, artificial intelligence requires a vast ecosystem of prompt engineers, ethicists, and cybersecurity analysts. Furthermore, the immense productivity gains generated by algorithmic efficiency lower consumer prices, freeing disposable capital to fuel growth in personal services, healthcare, and creative arts.

[Paragraf 4 - Conclusion]: In conclusion, while targeted public policies and retraining programs are indispensable during this transitional epoch, fears of systemic technological obsolescence are fundamentally unfounded. Artificial intelligence serves to augment human potential rather than extinguish it.

Model 3 · Problem & Solution: Metropolitan Traffic Congestion 286 Kata · Band 8.5

[Paragraf 1 - Introduction & Thesis]: In contemporary metropolises across the globe, escalating traffic gridlock has evolved from a minor inconvenience into a catastrophic economic and public health crisis. This essay will examine the twin catalysts of unchecked private vehicle reliance and inadequate suburban transit networks, before outlining targeted fiscal and infrastructural interventions that municipal authorities must undertake.

[Paragraf 2 - Body 1 (PEEL - Root Causes)]: The principal driver of chronic urban congestion is the entrenched cultural and physical dependence on personal automobiles, exacerbated by fragmented city planning. In many sprawling urban peripheries, residential developments are constructed without synchronized rapid-transit links, effectively compelling citizens to drive for everyday errands. Furthermore, artificially suppressed road-usage fees have long disincentivized commuters from seeking alternative transit. As a direct consequence, arterial ring roads suffer chronic overcapacity during peak morning and evening hours, causing staggering productivity losses due to wasted travel time and severe respiratory ailments from localized particulate emissions.

[Paragraf 3 - Body 2 (PEEL - Integrated Solutions)]: To mitigate this crisis effectively, city administrations must pursue a coordinated carrot-and-stick approach. First, governments should introduce dynamic electronic congestion pricing zones in central business districts, directly increasing the cost of single-occupancy vehicle trips during rush hour. Crucially, the fiscal revenue accrued from these levies should be ring-fenced exclusively to subsidize high-frequency light rail networks and protected cycling thoroughfares. The success of London's congestion charge demonstrates that financial deterrents coupled with dependable mass transit can drastically curtail private vehicle volume.

[Paragraf 4 - Conclusion]: In conclusion, urban gridlock is primarily the byproduct of automobile-centric urban design and transit neglect. By implementing robust congestion charges and reinvesting those funds into world-class public transportation, municipal authorities can foster sustainable, livable cities.
