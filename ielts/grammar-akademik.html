Grammar Akademik Pengungkit Skor (Band 7.0+)

Kriteria penilaian resmi Grammatical Range & Accuracy di IELTS Writing dan Speaking tidak mencari peserta yang hanya menggunakan kalimat sederhana (*simple sentences*) tanpa salah. Penguji mencari keberanian menggunakan variasi kalimat kompleks tingkat tinggi (*complex sentence structures*). Modul ini membedah 6 formula tata bahasa akademik dari buku Complete IELTS Bands 6.5–7.5 (Cambridge University Press).

Pola 1 · Complete IELTS Hal. 113

🌟 1. Cleft Sentences: Pola Penekanan Gagasan Tingkat Dewa

Emphasis Technique

Cleft sentence adalah teknik membelah satu kalimat menjadi dua klausa untuk memberikan penekanan khusus (*special focus*) pada informasi terpenting. Kalimat ini langsung mendongkrak kesan kecanggihan bahasa Anda di Writing Task 2 dan Speaking Part 3:

A. Pola What-Clause: What + Subject + Verb + is/was + ...
Kalimat Biasa (Band 5):

"People don't think about the level of planning involved in urban zoning."

Cleft Sentence Band 7.5 (Cambridge p. 113):

"What people fail to consider is the immense level of planning involved in urban zoning."

B. Pola It-Cleft: It is/was + Noun/Phrase + that/who + ...
Kalimat Biasa (Band 5):

"Lack of funding causes the failure of public transport projects."

It-Cleft Band 7.5 (Cambridge p. 113):

"It is a lack of sustainable funding that primarily undermines public transit initiatives."

C. Pola All-Clause: All that + Subject + Verb + is/was + ...
Kalimat Biasa (Band 5):

"We only need government political will to stop plastic pollution."

All-Cleft Band 7.5:

"All that is required to curb plastic pollution is concerted political commitment."

💬 2. Attitude Adverbials: Kata Keterangan Pembuka Sikap Penulis

Formal Sentence Starters

Menurut buku referensi Cambridge, Attitude adverbial adalah kata keterangan yang ditempatkan di awal kalimat dan selalu diikuti tanda koma (,). Fungsinya untuk menunjukkan sikap dan penilaian analitis penulis terhadap fakta yang disampaikan:

Kategori Sikap Attitude Adverbials (Cambridge p. 112) Contoh Penggunaan Nyata dalam Esai
Keterkejutan / Anomali Data Surprisingly, Remarkably, Curiously "Surprisingly, modern consumers increasingly favor eco-friendly packaging despite higher price tags."
Kekecewaan / Masalah Nyata Sadly, Regrettably, Disappointingly "Regrettably, few educational institutions allocate adequate budgets for hands-on scientific research."
Kejelasan Bukti / Logika Clearly, Obviously, Undeniably, Evidentially "Undeniably, artificial intelligence is transforming contemporary labor markets."
Objektivitas / Hipotesis Theoretically, Hypothetically, Logically "Theoretically, solar power could meet the energy demands of entire metropolitan areas."

⛓️ 3. Cause & Effect Connectors: Merangkai Sebab-Akibat Kompleks

Writing Task 1 & 2 Essential

Peserta Band 5 hanya tahu kata "because" dan "so". Peserta Band 7+ menguasai variasi frasa kausalitas formal yang mengalir mulus:

1. due to / owing to / because of

Wajib diikuti Noun Phrase atau Gerund (-ing), BUKAN klausa lengkap (Subject + Verb):

"Children may be neglected due to their parents working excessively long hours."
2. with the result / effect that

Digunakan untuk menyambungkan klausa konsekuensi langsung:

"The government introduced tax incentives, with the result that renewable energy investments doubled."
3. result in vs result from (Sering Tertukar!)

• Result in = Menyebabkan / berujung pada (A menyebabkan B).
• Result from = Diakibatkan oleh (B diakibatkan oleh A).

"Heavy vehicle emissions result in severe smog."
4. consequently / in consequence

Adverb transisi formal yang ditaruh di awal kalimat baru atau setelah titik koma:

"The river was contaminated; consequently, fishing was prohibited."

🎲 4. Conditionals & Hypothesising (Kalimat Pengandaian Kompleks)

Speaking Part 3 & Task 2

Penguji Speaking Part 3 sangat sering memancing Anda dengan pertanyaan hipotesis: "What would happen if robots replaced teachers?". Gunakan struktur Second, Third, atau Mixed Conditionals untuk menunjukkan penguasaan tata bahasa tingkat tinggi:

2nd Conditional (Imajiner Sekarang):

"If universities offered tuition-free degrees, social mobility would increase dramatically."

If + Past, would + V1
3rd Conditional (Penyesalan/Pengandaian Masa Lalu):

"If environmental regulations had been enacted sooner, coral reefs would not have suffered irreversible bleaching."

If + Past Perfect, would have + V3
Mixed Conditional (Puncak Penguasaan Band 8.0):

"If scientists had not discovered penicillin in 1928, millions of people would still be dying from minor bacterial infections today."

Past Condition → Present Result

⚖️ 5. Hedging: Seni Bahasa Ilmiah yang Berimbang (Cautious Language)

Academic Tone

Salah satu kesalahan terbesar peserta Indonesia adalah membuat klaim mutlak 100%: "Social media always destroys teenagers' mental health." Di lingkungan akademik Cambridge, pernyataan mutlak ini dianggap **tidak ilmiah** (*unsubstantiated generalization*). Anda wajib menggunakan Hedging untuk mengatur tingkat kepastian:

Tingkat Kepastian Tinggi (Very Certain)
  • • "There is little doubt that..."
  • • "Space exploration is bound to continue."
  • • "There is every likelihood that..."
Tingkat Moderat (Likely / Probable)
  • • "Governments are likely to reach an accord."
  • • "It tends to correlate with..."
  • • "It is arguably one of the most..."
Tingkat Rendah / Ragu (Unlikely / Doubt)
  • • "It is highly unlikely that..."
  • • "Observers very much doubt whether..."
  • • "There is only a slim possibility that..."
